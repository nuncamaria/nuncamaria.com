About me

I started in illustration and graphic design, where I learned to approach problems visually and from unexpected angles.

Narciso Yepes. Music Awards.

Visual Identity · 2026

Context

The Narciso Yepes Awards are annual music awards in the Region of Murcia, named after the Lorca-born guitarist Narciso Yepes, one of the most important figures in Spanish music. The goal of the project was to design the posters for its 10th edition, giving the event a fresh, contemporary visual identity that strengthens its standing as a cultural landmark in the region.

The concept of my solution grew out of studying his thinking and his relationship with music. Yepes understood music as a form of transcendence, and it was his way of connecting with his faith.

Concept: Music lifts you up.

Murcia Natural

UI web design · 2026

Context

Design of an interactive proposal: a website dedicated to the natural areas of the Region of Murcia.

The idea is built around one central tension: the landscape can be explored, but not organized; it can be known, but not altered; it can be visited, but not exploited. This tension carries over into the website through a hard, direct and unornamented aesthetic that refuses to soften the image of these natural spaces.

Concept: Raw nature.

I'm rich

Advertising campaign · 2025

Context

A graphic communication campaign created to encourage traditional, local shopping in Murcia and its outlying districts.

The proposal grows out of the idea of wealth and abundance: helping people realize how truly rich they are for having everything close by. Through humor, irony and Murcian identity, we reinterpret wealth not as something material, but as the everyday abundance that surrounds us in our neighborhoods: the bakery that has always been there, the greengrocer you trust, the hardware store where they know you by name.

Concept: I'm rich because I have everything without leaving my neighborhood.

Damaqua

Branding · 2026

Context

A corporate identity project for a fictional water supply company based in the Region of Murcia. It covers the naming, the brand concept, the definition of its personality and the visual identity system.

The concept is built around the demijohn (damajuana), an iconic vessel associated with storing water: tradition, containment, essence. For the name, “aqua” was added, as it is the Latin root of “agua”: clarity, purity, universality.
