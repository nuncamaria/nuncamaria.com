👀 About me

I started in illustration and graphic design, where I learned to approach problems visually and from unexpected angles.

Curious about how interfaces are built, I moved into front-end development and then Android, where I worked for three years in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

Today I bring both sides together in UI/UX, designing with an understanding of how things get built.

I believe people are in the world to observe and listen. That's how I see life. After that, we can figure out what to do with it. I care about people and the problems we go through and I try to come up with solutions in line with my values.
