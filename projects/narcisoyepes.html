Narciso Yepes. Music Awards.

Context

The Narciso Yepes Awards are annual music awards in the Region of Murcia, named after the Lorca-born guitarist Narciso Yepes, one of the most important figures in Spanish music. The goal of the project was to design the posters for its 10th edition, giving the event a fresh, contemporary visual identity that strengthens its standing as a cultural landmark in the region.

The concept of my solution grew out of studying his thinking and his relationship with music. Yepes understood music as a form of transcendence, and it was his way of connecting with his faith.

Concept: Music lifts you up.
