Damaqua

Context

A corporate identity project for a fictional water supply company based in the Region of Murcia. It covers the naming, the brand concept, the definition of its personality and the visual identity system.

The concept is built around the demijohn (damajuana), an iconic vessel associated with storing water: tradition, containment, essence. For the name, “aqua” was added, as it is the Latin root of “agua”: clarity, purity, universality.

Role
Graphic Designer
Client
Fictional client, a class project at the School of Design
